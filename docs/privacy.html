Stored here.Sent nowhere.

Margo 0.1 has no account, server, analytics, advertising, AI, sync, crash-reporting SDK, or content upload.

What is stored

Note text, marker colors, note order, and timestamps live in ~/Library/Application Support/Margo/notes.json.

What leaves your Mac

Nothing from Margo. Version 0.1 makes no network requests. Opening the feedback link hands that action to your default browser.

Launch at Login

The optional Launch at Login switch is off by default and managed by macOS through Service Management.

Removing your data

Deleting Margo.app does not remove your notes file. Copy anything you want to keep, then delete the Margo folder in Application Support.
